Instrument samples used by the recap music.
Piano: Salamander Grand Piano V2 by Alexander Holm, CC BY 3.0 (https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/), via github.com/Tonejs/audio
All other instruments: FluidR3_GM by Frank Wen, CC BY 3.0, rendered by github.com/gleitz/midi-js-soundfonts
Samples were trimmed, converted to mono MP3 and reduced to one note every minor third.
